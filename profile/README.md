# Welcome to the Energybench Association 🌍⚡

We’re thrilled to have you here and hope you find what you're looking for 🙌!

**Energybench** is an open initiative to build a **global benchmark for energy generation data** for AI and data-driven energy meteorology.

Our goal is to make renewable energy generation data and meteorological data easier to **discover, standardize, combine, and use** for scientific benchmarking and model development.
By providing open data pipelines, metadata standards, and benchmark tooling, RenewBench aims to support transparent and reproducible research at the intersection of **energy systems** and **weather/climate data science**.

🔗 Check out our official website: [www.energybench.org](https://www.energybench.org)!

📚 For more information, see:
- the [project call website](https://helmholtz-imaging.de/project/renewbench-a-global-benchmark-for-renewable-energy-generation/)

## Repositories

### Building the database

#### [`RenewBench-Crawler`](https://github.com/Energybench-Association/RenewBench-Crawler)
Downloaders and processing pipelines for raw renewable energy and meteorological data.

#### [`stac-extension-energy`](https://github.com/Energybench-Association/stac-extension-energy)
A proposal for a **STAC extension** to describe metadata for energy generation and storage time series.

### Using the database

#### [`energybench`](https://github.com/Energybench-Association/energybench)
The official package for accessing, parsing and downloading from the EnergyBench database.

#### [`benchmarks`](https://github.com/Energybench-Association/benchmarks)
The collection of all official EnergyBench benchmarks. New proposals can easily be submitted via [the website](https://www.energybench.org/benchmarks/).
