# Welcome to the RenewBench Association 🌍⚡

We’re thrilled to have you here and hope you find what you're looking for 🙌!

**RenewBench** is an open initiative to build a **global benchmark for renewable energy generation data** for AI and data-driven energy meteorology.

Our goal is to make renewable energy generation data and meteorological data easier to **discover, standardize, combine, and use** for scientific benchmarking and model development.
By providing open data pipelines, metadata standards, and benchmark tooling, RenewBench aims to support transparent and reproducible research at the intersection of **energy systems** and **weather/climate data science**.

🔗 For more information, check out the [project website](https://helmholtz-imaging.de/project/renewbench-a-global-benchmark-for-renewable-energy-generation/).

## Repositories

### [`RenewBench-Crawler`](https://github.com/RenewBench-Association/RenewBench-Crawler)
Downloaders and processing pipelines for raw renewable energy and meteorological data.

### [`stac-extension-energy`](https://github.com/RenewBench-Association/stac-extension-energy)
A proposal for a **STAC extension** to describe metadata for energy generation and storage time series.
